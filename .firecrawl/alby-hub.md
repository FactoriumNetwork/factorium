# Alby Hub is a lightning wallet like no other.  Connect and use it in dozens of apps.

### Your keys, your lightning

Alby Hub is truly yours — only you own the keys to your bitcoin.

Run it where you prefer: one-click setup with Alby Cloud, Umbrel, Start9, or any in other environment you want.

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/ZBbW9xG5436lT62KDYkimctKZk.png?width=2254&height=2763)

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/tq7UTfPeZqcwGfjYMuMijwbeI.png?width=1920&height=1670)

### No extra fees

Pay only the network fees. Alby adds nothing on top.

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/bviRX85r4kRWTELDVSITxY56kE.png?width=1482&height=1207)

### Onboard your friends

Create sub-wallets for family, friends, or co-workers.

### Integrated app store

Discover apps you can use with your Hub in an integrated app store within the app.

Connect and enjoy self-custodial payments anywhere — websites, mobile apps, desktop apps, games, plugins and extensions, backend services, CLI tools and more.

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/BBJ5qVQWaFweoui83GUik5WRQg.png?width=1631&height=2168)

### Simple permissions management

You're in full control of app permissions you use Alby Hub with.

Set app permissions and budgets by day, week, month, or year.

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/HWb7hxyrIzLCxaPLVb4ILcBYPA.png?width=1620&height=2168)

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/lSoB9dlKvbMPSyPgrXO8SG1qIQ.png?width=1920&height=1670)

### Auto-swaps

Swap bitcoin between on-chain and lightning network inside the app.
